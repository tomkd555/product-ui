search Computer Science > Human-Computer Interaction arXiv:2605.15124 (cs) [Submitted on 14 May 2026] Title: Usable but Conventional: An Empirical Study on the UX of AI-Generated Interface Prototypes 
Authors: Karoline Romero , Igor Wiese , Renato Balancieiri , Gislaine Camila Leal , Guilherme Guerino View a PDF of the paper titled Usable but Conventional: An Empirical Study on the UX of 
AI-Generated Interface Prototypes, by Karoline Romero and Igor Wiese and Renato Balancieiri and Gislaine Camila Leal and Guilherme Guerino View PDF HTML (experimental) Abstract: This paper 
investigates User Experience (UX) with prototypes generated by Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) tools. An empirical survey with 92 participants evaluated AI-generated and human-created 
prototypes without prior identification of authorship. We measured UX using the UEQ-S, covering pragmatic and hedonic dimensions. Results indicate positive evaluations in pragmatic aspects, such as 
usability and efficiency, and neutral or negative evaluations in hedonic aspects, including originality and innovation. We concluded that GenAI can produce functional interfaces but tends to 
reinforce visual and structural patterns that affect perceptions of originality. Comments: Paper accepted for presentation at SEMISH 2026 - 53rd Integrated Software and Hardware Seminar (Congress of 
the Brazilian Computer Society - 2026) Subjects: Human-Computer Interaction (cs.HC) Cite as: arXiv:2605.15124 [cs.HC] (or arXiv:2605.15124v1 [cs.HC] for this version) https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.
